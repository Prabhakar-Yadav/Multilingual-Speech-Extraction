Turning Bengali Doctor Calls into Medical Information

A plain-English summary of what was built, how, and what it produces · VaultStack
The goal: Take a recorded phone call between a doctor and patient (spoken in Bengali) and automatically pull out the useful medical information — what's wrong with the patient, what tests or procedures are involved, and which medicines are mentioned — so a hospital can act on it without listening to every call.
The challenge we hit: The recordings are very low quality — like a muffled, far-away phone line. The crisp sounds that let you tell one word from another are simply missing from the audio. A person could tell "someone is speaking Bengali," but not make out the exact words. This made the job genuinely hard.

What we did — step by step

We didn't get it on the first try. We improved the approach in stages, testing a better tool each time:

AttemptWhat we triedResult
1Standard speech-to-text softwareGibberish — invented random words
2Cleaned the audio (removed background hum, boosted the voice)Slightly clearer, still unusable
3Switched to AI trained specifically on the Bengali languageRecovered real sentences, but messy
4Tried AI trained on Bengali medical conversationsCaught medical phrasing, still unreliable
5Used a powerful cloud AI (OpenAI Whisper "Large", via Groq)Finally clear, readable English
6Fed that to a smart language model to read it and list the medical termsReal diagnoses, procedures & symptoms
7Added a "confidence check" so the AI can't make up medicine namesHonest results — no invented data

The final system (simple version)

Step 1 — Listen: a powerful speech AI converts the Bengali audio into text and English.
Step 2 — Understand: a language AI reads the English and picks out the medical terms.
Step 3 — Verify: each term comes with a confidence level, so unreliable guesses are flagged, never trusted blindly.

What you get — for every call

One clean file (RESULT.json) per call, containing:

FieldWhat it is
transcript_bengaliThe call written out in Bengali
translation_englishThe English translation
medical_termsDiagnoses, procedures, tests, symptoms, medicines — extracted automatically

The honest bottom line

It works. From audio that earlier produced only gibberish, we now reliably get the patient's conditions, procedures and symptoms in plain English.

One limitation: exact medicine names could not be recovered from these particular recordings — they're either spoken only as "medicine" or lost to the poor audio quality. This is a recording-quality issue, not a system flaw. With clearer recordings (better microphone / phone line), the same system will also capture medicine names.

Method: Groq Whisper Large V3 (speech) + Llama 3.3 70B (extraction), with confidence-checking. Full per-call results are in the numbered folders (1–4) as RESULT.json. · VaultStack · 2026
